# Hiring & Interview Kit (marketing roles)
Read the active client pack — stage and motion determine the right hire (a $50M sales-led company hiring "a marketer" usually needs demand gen or PMM first; a PLG company usually needs growth/lifecycle; challenge vague reqs before writing them).

## 1. Scorecard before job description
For any role define: mission (one sentence), 3–5 outcomes for the first year WITH numbers (not responsibilities — "build the webinar program to 40 opps/quarter" not "own webinars"), and 6–8 competencies split must-have/trainable. The persona charters (persona-vp-growth, persona-product-marketing-director, etc.) double as senior-role scorecard seeds. The JD is the scorecard rewritten for candidates + honest stage context ("you'll have no team for 6 months").

## 2. Structured interview loop (same questions per role, every candidate, scored 1–4 against the scorecard)
Standard loop for marketing roles: screen (motivation + trajectory) → hiring-manager deep-dive (past-behavior evidence per competency: "walk me through the campaign you're proudest of — what was YOUR part, what did it produce, what would you redo") → work sample → cross-functional interview (sales leader for demand/PMM roles — non-negotiable) → values/team.
Legal guardrail: questions probe competencies only — nothing touching protected characteristics (age, family plans, health, origin, etc.), and the same loop for every candidate isn't just good practice, it's the defensible record. When in doubt, the client's HR/counsel reviews the loop before use.
Question discipline: past behavior over hypotheticals; numbers over adjectives ("what was the cost per opp?" — comfort with that question is itself signal); probe the "we": "what specifically did you do?"

## 3. Work samples (the highest-signal hour — keep them fair)
Time-boxed (≤2–3 hrs), on a fictional or public scenario (never free work on the client's real backlog), compensated for candidates at IC level where norms expect it. By role: demand gen → critique this funnel data + plan next quarter; PMM → positioning teardown of a public product + messaging for one persona; content/SEO → brief + argue a priority call; ops → diagnose this messy report. Score against pre-written criteria: judgment and reasoning over polish.

## 4. Debrief discipline
Scores submitted independently BEFORE discussion (anchoring is the killer); debrief same-day or next-day; hiring manager decides — votes advise, they don't tally; disagreement on a must-have competency = no-hire or another data point, never "they'll grow into it" for must-haves. Reference calls done by the hiring manager, asking the scorecard's top-2 competencies specifically and "would you hire them again for THIS role?"
Log every hire's scorecard + 12-month outcome; the comparison is how the kit itself improves.
