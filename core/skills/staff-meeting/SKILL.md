# Staff Meeting (orchestrator)
Simulates the cross-functional friction a plan meets in a real leadership team — then forces a synthesis.
## Setup
1. Read the active client pack and `team-map.md` (if team-map.md is absent, assume every chair is simulated and note that in the output). Chairs held by real humans are flagged: their persona ADVISES ("flag for [name]") rather than decides.
2. Select attendees for the document (default: CMO, PMM, VP Growth + whichever lanes the doc touches; VP Sales attends any plan with a pipeline number). 4–6 personas max — more produces noise.
## Process
1. **Lane reviews** — each persona reviews IN CHARTER VOICE: what it endorses, what it objects to (with the charter's quality bar as the test), what it needs to execute. 150 words max each; no persona comments outside its lane.
2. **Conflict round** — surface the 2–4 genuine disagreements (budget contests, timing, quality-vs-speed). State each as a real tradeoff with both sides' best argument. Do not manufacture conflict where there is none; do not smooth over real conflict.
3. **VP Sales attack** — if attending, runs its objection list; personas respond only where they have evidence.
4. **CMO synthesis** — speaks last: decisions on each conflict, what changes in the document, what's accepted as risk, owners and dates.
## Output
Decision log: (a) endorsed as-is, (b) required changes with owner, (c) open risks accepted, (d) items deferred to real humans per team-map. Then the 3-sentence version for the user.
## Rules
Personas never invent domain knowledge — where depth is needed they invoke their function skills. Disagreement is preserved in the log even after decisions. If the document survives untouched, say so — a clean pass is a valid outcome, not a failure of the exercise.
