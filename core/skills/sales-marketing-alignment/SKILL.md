# Sales–Marketing Alignment Operating System
Read the active client pack. Definitions live in `metrics-baseline.md` — this skill creates/enforces the agreement AROUND them; never fork a second definitions list.

## 1. The definitions treaty (do first, in one room)
Marketing + sales leadership + RevOps agree in writing: MQL, SQL, opportunity, marketing-sourced, marketing-influenced, recycled, disqualified — each with the objective criteria and the CRM field that proves it. Sign-off recorded in `metrics-baseline.md` with date. Rule: definitions change only at quarter boundaries, by the same group.

## 2. The bilateral SLA (both directions, or it's a stick not a deal)
- **Marketing commits:** SQL volume per month (from `pipeline-math`), quality floor (SQL→opp conversion ≥ agreed %), lead context delivered (what they did, what to say).
- **Sales commits:** follow-up speed (first touch on SQLs within 1 business day; 5-minute target on demo requests), minimum touches before recycling (typ. 5–8 over 2 weeks), disposition hygiene (every lead gets a CRM outcome — no black holes).
- **Recycle loop:** rejected leads return with a reason code; marketing reports the reason mix monthly and fixes real quality issues rather than defending them.
Publish the SLA one page long. Longer agreements don't get read; unread agreements don't get honored.

## 3. Operating rhythm
- **Weekly pipeline council (30 min):** SLA scoreboard both directions, this week's handoff disputes resolved against the definitions (not against anecdotes), next 2 weeks' campaign/event calendar so sales is never surprised.
- **Monthly:** funnel review — conversion by source vs. SLA floors; joint decision on what to scale/kill.
- **Quarterly:** definitions review, target re-plan with `pipeline-math`, win-loss readout (`win-loss-program`) heard jointly.
Escalation protocol: disputes unresolved in council go to CMO+CRO with the data, decided within a week, decision logged. The protocol existing is what keeps disputes small.

## 4. Failure patterns to name early
"The leads are bad" without disposition data = SLA breach on sales' side, handled as such. Marketing shipping volume that misses the quality floor = marketing's breach, ditto. Attribution fights = route to `attribution-diagnostics`, and report sourced + influenced side by side so credit isn't zero-sum.
